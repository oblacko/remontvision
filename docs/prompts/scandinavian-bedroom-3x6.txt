SCANDINAVIAN DESIGN DIRECTION:

Create a contemporary Scandinavian interior that is warm,
functional, tactile and visually distinctive.

Combine:
- Clean, practical furniture forms.
- Natural wood and honest material textures.
- Soft textiles and comfortable upholstery.
- A deliberate colour composition.
- One clear focal point.
- Thoughtful wall styling.
- Layered lighting.
- A restrained collection of meaningful decorative objects.
- Radiator covers integrated into the overall design where required.

Avoid interpreting Scandinavian design as an almost empty white room.
Use calm negative space, but make the main visible areas feel resolved.
